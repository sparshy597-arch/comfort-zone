# Comfort Zone

A social-confidence app prototype: one real-world social challenge a day, sized to how anxious you actually feel.

**The loop:** predict how anxious you'll feel (1–10), do the challenge (or a gentler version), then rate how it really felt. Over time you see the gap between the fear and the moment.

## Open it

- **App:** `index.html`, an Apple-style glass web app (light/dark mode, 4 wallpapers)
- **Website:** `website.html`, the scrolling landing page

Download or clone the repo and double-click `index.html`. No install, no build step.

Click **"Explore with a sample account"** to see it with a week of history.

## What's inside

- Welcome, sign-up and a 7-scenario calibration
- **Today:** the challenge, the anxiety prediction slider, the reflection and the predicted-vs-actual result
- **Progress:** predicted vs actual chart, comfort rings for all 7 areas, history
- **Challenges:** all 35 challenges across 7 areas and 5 tiers, each with its own ladder
- **You:** goals, theme, wallpaper, account

## Notes

- This is a front-end prototype. Data is saved only in your browser (localStorage); there is no server.
- The sample account (Sam) and their history are made-up demo data.
- Photo credits are listed at the bottom of `website.html` and in `assets/credits.json`.
